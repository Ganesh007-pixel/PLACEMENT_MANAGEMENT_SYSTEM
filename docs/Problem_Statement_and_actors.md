 Placement Management System
Problem statement
The placement process in a college involves students, company recruiters, the TPO/Placement Officer, and Faculty Coordinators.
Managing job opportunities, applications, and application status manually can be difficult and time-consuming.
Students may not know which jobs are available or what is happening with their applications.
Recruiters and college staff also need an easy way to monitor applications.
As a Student, I want to register and log in to the system and maintain my personal, academic, and resume details.
I want to view available job and internship opportunities, apply for suitable opportunities, and check my application status. 
I should also receive notifications about my applications and status updates.
As a Recruiter (Company HR), I want to log in and view the applications received for the jobs posted by my company.
I want to check the status of these applications so that I can manage the recruitment process easily.
As a TPO/Placement Officer, I want to manage and monitor the job opportunities available on the platform.
I also want to monitor student applications and their status during the placement process.
As a Faculty Coordinator, I want to log in and monitor the application status of students so that I can guide and support them during the placement process.
The system should use an Email/SMS service to send notifications to students and recruiters about job applications and application status updates.
A Scheduler should automatically update application status records at regular intervals without requiring a user to do it manually.
Therefore, the Placement Tracker System will provide one common platform for students, recruiters, TPOs, and faculty coordinators to manage and track placement activities.
It will make the placement process more organized, simple, and easy to track for everyone involved..
